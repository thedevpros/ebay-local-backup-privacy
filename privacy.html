Privacy Policy - eBay Local Backup

eBay Local Backup is a private application used by the account owner to create local backups of their own eBay seller data.

Information accessed

The application may access eBay account, listing, inventory, business policy, image and related seller information authorised by the account owner.

How information is used

Information is accessed solely for the purpose of creating and maintaining a local backup of the account owner's eBay data.

Storage

Backup data and authentication credentials are stored locally on systems controlled by the account owner.

Sharing and sale of data

eBay user data accessed by this application is not sold, rented, or shared with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Access and deletion

The account owner controls the locally stored backup data and may delete it at any time. eBay authorisation may also be revoked through the account owner's eBay account.

Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect locally stored credentials and data from unauthorised access.
